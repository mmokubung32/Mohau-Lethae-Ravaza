# Content Guide — what the studio still needs to supply

This site is deliberately built to hold better content than exists today without a redesign. Here's what would upgrade it, roughly in order of impact.

## Highest impact

- **Higher-resolution artwork photography.** The current images are extracted from a PDF catalogue (screenshots/scans), which is why some show visible framing, wall texture, or slightly uneven lighting. Clean, evenly lit photographs — ideally shot square-on against a neutral background, in natural or diffused light — would noticeably lift the whole site, since the artwork carries almost the entire visual identity.
- **Confirmed dimensions for `Smangele`.** Currently flagged in the UI as unconfirmed (source listed 700cm × 600cm, almost certainly a typo).
- **Clarified pricing for the Black Panther Series.** Currently listed as R6,000 for the series of 3 — confirm whether that's per-piece or for the set, since the detail page currently states it as a series price only.
- **Access to the two linked artist statements** (for "The People of Vaal" and "Black Panthers — The Trans-Atlantic Squad") — both link to Google Docs that aren't publicly viewable. Sharing these (or their text directly) lets those two detail pages carry a real statement instead of a generic note.

## Studio & process photography

- The artist at work — studio environment, hands mid-cross-hatch, materials (pen, ink, lino/dry-point tools).
- Process shots for at least one piece from blank surface to finished work, even a handful of stages — this is exactly the kind of material that makes the "handmade, not templated" positioning credible rather than asserted.
- A proper artist portrait for the Story page (currently that page doesn't use one, leaning on artwork alone — a portrait would help, but only if it fits the visual tone; happy to advise once one exists).

## Wearable art (needed before that section can go live)

- Once the first hand-painted garments exist: front, back, and close-up detail photography per piece; fabric/material information; care instructions; confirmed sizing.

## Business/logistics details needed before checkout can be built

- Preferred courier or shipping partner for standard parcels (small originals, prints) — see `ARCHITECTURE.md`'s shipping section.
- How framed/large-artwork shipping should actually be handled today (courier quote per piece? collection-only for now?) — this determines whether Phase 9's "fragile shipping" flow is fully automated or manual-quote at launch.
- A real studio contact email and phone number to replace the placeholder `hello@mohaulethae.art` used throughout this prototype.
- Confirmation of the Facebook profile URL if it should be linked from the Contact page (mentioned by the client but not confirmed as a public, correct URL — currently left as text-only, unlinked).

## Nice to have, lower urgency

- Exhibition photography (installation shots from Sasol New Signatures, Back to Basics) for the Exhibitions page.
- Any press coverage or write-ups, if they exist, for a future press/mentions section.
- Testimonials from collectors, once there are sales to reference — not fabricated in the interim.

## What not to send

Please don't send finished marketing copy or a "brand guidelines" document from a general template — the current copy is written specifically from the verified biography and the Sasol statement, and generic replacement copy would undo that. If something reads wrong or inaccurate, flag the specific line rather than resupplying wholesale text, so corrections stay grounded in fact rather than drifting toward generic art-website language.
