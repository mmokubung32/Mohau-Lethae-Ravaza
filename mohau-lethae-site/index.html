South African artist, Orange Farm — printmaking, portraiture, collective memory

Lines built one hatch at a time, until a person looks back at you.

Mohau “Ravaza” Lethae works in cross-hatch and stipple line, mixed media and paint — trained in lino cut and dry point at Artist Proof Studio, and a 2025 Sasol New Signatures finalist at the Pretoria Art Museum.

Sasol New Signatures

Abantu Literacy League

Facilitates drawing lessons and art competitions for children.

Mohau works between print discipline and paint — the same cross-hatched, hand-built line running through portraits of elders, self-interrogation, and pieces built directly from social commentary and collective memory. He currently lives in Orange Farm, where his mentor, artist Tawanda Mhandu, taught him the value of archiving and making original work.
