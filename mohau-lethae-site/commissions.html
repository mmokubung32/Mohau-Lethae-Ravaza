Commissions

Commission an original piece

Portraits, community or family pieces, and larger works can be discussed directly with the studio. Every commission begins with a conversation, not a fixed package — pricing depends on size, medium and subject.

How it works

1. Enquire

Share references, size, and what the piece is for. The studio will confirm feasibility and an estimated price range.

2. Confirm

Once scope and price are agreed, the piece is scheduled into the studio's working queue.

3. Deliver

Mohau shares progress where relevant. Framing, shipping and collection are arranged on completion.
